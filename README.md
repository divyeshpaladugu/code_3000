# Ethics Personal Statement

After reading Chapter 5 of *Ethical Machines*, I think ethics in technology is about making sure machines and AI are used in ways that help people and do not cause harm. Just because technology is advanced or useful does not mean it should be used without thinking about the consequences. The people creating these systems need to be responsible for how they work and how they affect others.

I think ethics should be part of the process from the beginning, not something people only care about after problems happen. Fairness, privacy, accountability, and transparency all matter because technology can shape people’s lives in serious ways. Chapter 5 made me think more about how important it is for humans to stay in control and take responsibility for the choices made when using machines and AI.
